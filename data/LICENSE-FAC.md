# Federal Audit Clearinghouse data: terms

`findings.csv` and `findings_text.csv` are a subset of the Federal Audit Clearinghouse (FAC) public data,
downloaded on 14 November 2026 from https://www.fac.gov/data/download/ (files `2025-ay-findings.csv` and
`2025-ay-findings_text.csv`).

The FAC states on https://www.fac.gov/data/ (read 14 November 2026):

> The data collected by the FAC is free to use and in the public domain.

The FAC is run by the U.S. General Services Administration. The findings are written by independent auditors in
Single Audit reports under the Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200, Subpart F); they are not Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) audits.
